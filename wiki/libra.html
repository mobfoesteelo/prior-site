$LIBRA / cryptogate

entry/07 · cycle/12 · february 14, 2025 · status: archived · ongoing investigation

summary

The $LIBRA cryptocurrency scandal — known in Argentine media as Cryptogate or Libragate — was a memecoin rug pull launched on February 14, 2025, in connection with an endorsement from Argentine President Javier Milei. The scandal led to roughly $251 million in retail losses, affected approximately 44,000 investors, and triggered an ongoing federal investigation in Argentina.

timeline

  • Friday, February 14, 2025, 6:58pm Argentina time — Delaware-registered Kelsier Ventures launches the $LIBRA token without prior public announcement, as part of a project called Viva La Libertad.
  • Within minutes — President Javier Milei tweets endorsing the project to his 3M+ followers. Token price spikes.
  • Within hours — Kelsier-controlled wallets withdraw $87M+ in liquidity from the LP. Price collapses ~95%. 70% of total token supply was held by the project's creators.
  • A small number of accounts realize $70M–$100M in profits in those hours.
  • Days later — Milei publicly distances himself, claiming he was "not aware of the details." Argentine opposition launches impeachment proceedings.
  • April 2025 — Hayden Davis (Kelsier CEO) admits on camera to "sniping" the launch. Quote: "our goal was, can we take enough liquidity off to get all the snipers out."
  • April 2025 — Bubblemaps publishes blockchain analysis showing the same wallet pattern previously used for the $MELANIA launch.
  • April 2026 — The New York Times reports that Milei's official call logs show multiple phone exchanges with operator Mauricio Novelli the night of the launch — both before and after Milei's endorsement tweet — contradicting his claim of non-involvement.

key actors

  • Hayden Mark Davis — 28-year-old American crypto marketer, CEO of Kelsier Ventures. Also implicated in the $MELANIA insider scheme (~$99.6M extracted in 3 minutes), and in earlier projects $AIAI, $MATES, $M3M3 — each of which crashed ~95% shortly after launch. Cumulative profits across the schemes estimated at over $200 million. (Family background: father reportedly served prison time for counterfeiting; great-grandfather led a polygamist religious sect.)
  • Mauricio Novelli — Argentine entrepreneur, identified by federal investigators as a key intermediary between Milei and the Kelsier launch.
  • Javier Milei — Argentine president since December 2023. Milei's role remains the subject of an active federal probe.
  • Kelsier Ventures — Delaware-registered shell entity behind the launch. Davis runs it with his brother Gideon (25) and father Tom (54).

the quote that defined the cycle

"our goal was, can we take enough liquidity off to get all the snipers out." — Hayden Davis, on video

The phrasing is characteristic. The architect of the rug describes himself as the architect of the defense against snipers. His own snipers got there first. So did everyone else's.

why this matters to PRIOR

$LIBRA is the modern paradigm of the political-coin rug. A head of state, a one-night launch, a foreign shell entity, an immediate $87M LP withdrawal, a multi-day denial, then call logs surfacing a year later. The pattern is so clean it functions as a template — and templates get reused.

PRIOR's role here is not to outrage. The outrage was already taken. PRIOR's role is to file: cycle/12, archived, $251M, 44,000, the quote, the call logs. So that the next time it happens — and it will — the prior is already on record.
