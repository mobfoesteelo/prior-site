# PRIOR · MEME PROMPT DECK · v2 (CHARACTER + REMIXABLE)

The first deck (`MEMES.md`) is documentary/cinematic — heavy on receipts, low on character. Good for serious-finance-twitter, weak for memecoin virality.

This deck flips the formula:
- **Character-first**: PRIOR himself appears in most of these (hooded figure with phosphor-green CRT eye-glyphs)
- **Lighter tonal range**: dry-funny instead of dry-grim
- **Brighter / higher contrast**: pops in feed scroll
- **Remixable templates**: format-meme-friendly so people can adapt PRIOR to their own jokes

Style baseline (use across deck unless overridden):
> bright cyberpunk illustration with high contrast, phosphor-green (#00ff66) accents, charcoal-black backgrounds, slight CRT scanline overlay, simplified character art (think tarot-card meets retro-pixel), the PRIOR character is a hooded figure in a long charcoal trench coat with two glowing CRT-green eye-glyphs (`<0>` `<0>`) and no visible face — the same character in every meme so people learn to recognize him instantly. Square 1:1 unless noted.

Caption rules:
- All lowercase. Dry. No emojis. No hashtags. No `$PRIOR` cashtag (looks shilly).
- Land the joke in 8-15 words max in the image when text-heavy memes are needed.
- Tweet captions stay under 240 chars to leave room for thread context.

## NOTES FOR USE

- **Spin in clusters.** Generate 5-6 of these in a session, post 1 every 3-6 hours. Don't carpet-bomb.
- **Establish the character first.** Section I (1-5) is the priority — those build mascot recognition. Don't post Section IV before people know who the hooded figure is.
- **Mix with text-only posts.** A character meme works best when it follows or precedes a strong text post. Image-then-image-then-image kills engagement.
- **Watch which ones move.** Track which images get the most retweets/replies. Spin variations of the winners. The first deck didn't move — these will likely have variance too. Iterate.
- **Animate the winners.** When a static image lands, run it through Runway/Kling/Pika to make a 3-5 second loop. Animated content gets ~3x the engagement of static in 2026 CT.

The character (`PRIOR — hooded figure with `<0>` `<0>` phosphor eye-glyphs`) is the protagonist. Keep him consistent across every meme. After 10-15 posts, people will recognize the silhouette and the meme will work without explanation.

Brevity > complexity. Recognition > impression.
